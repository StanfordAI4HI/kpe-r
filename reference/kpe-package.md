# kpe: Estimating the Value of Personalized Treatment Assignment

Point estimate, confidence interval, and one-sided p-value for the
personalization effect – the expected outcome difference between the
best personalized policy and the best single treatment applied to
everyone. Implements the K-Fold Personalization Estimator of Li and
Brunskill (2026). Supports binary and multi-arm discrete treatments,
pluggable reward, contextual-policy, and best-arm learners, and is
robust to mild misspecification of the plug-in models.
